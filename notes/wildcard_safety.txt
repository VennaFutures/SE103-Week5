What * meant in my pattern:
The * in data*.txt means match any characters after data, so it matched data1.txt, data2.txt, and data3.txt.

Why I listed matches before copying:
I listed matches first with ls data*.txt to confirm exactly which files would be affected. This safety check prevents copying or deleting wrong files.

Why wildcards are dangerous with delete commands:
Wildcards are dangerous with rm because rm data*.txt will delete every file that matches instantly with no undo. If the pattern is too broad like rm *.txt you could delete your whole project.
